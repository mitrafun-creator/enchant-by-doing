# Welcome to the Enchant by Doing (EbD) Wiki

> **"Master your craft through action, not RNG."**

**Enchant by Doing (EbD)** is a total progression and enchanting overhaul for Minecraft (**NeoForge 1.21.1**), expanding upon the original *Enchanting System Overhaul (ESO)* by CmdrJane.

EbD removes the frustrating random number generator (RNG) and endless AFK XP mob farms from vanilla Minecraft, replacing them with a deep, Skyrim-inspired RPG progression system. By performing actual gameplay activities—mining, fighting, shooting, farming, chopping trees, and brewing/enchanting—you level up your skills, unlock celestial constellations in the night sky, gain powerful active and passive perks, and craft precise enchantments using tangible recipe ingredients.

1. **Learning by Doing (Action Progression):**
   * Experience is gained directly from the activities related to each skill.
   * Six core paths: **Miner**, **Warrior**, **Hunter**, **Lumberjack**, **Farmer**, and **Enchanter**.
   * Anti-abuse mechanics prevent player-placed block grinding or repetitive exploits.

2. **Celestial Constellation Sky GUI:**
   * Press `K` (customizable) to gaze into a cosmic star-filled sky.
   * Pan across glowing constellations connected by pulsing Norse/Elder Futhark runic glyph lines.
   * Unlock stars as your skill level rises, activating game-changing passive abilities and active perks.

3. **Deterministic, Discovery-Based Enchanting:**
   * Discover enchantments by exploring the world and finding ancient **Dusty Books**.
   * Enchant items using specific material recipes (diamonds, lapis, amethyst, mob drops) without random rerolls.
   * Smart conflict resolution automatically swaps mutually exclusive enchantments on tables and anvils.

4. **Extended Sandbox Mechanics:**
   * Over a dozen new enchantments for weapons, tools, bows, crossbows, and even **boats** (amphibious gliders, submarines, motorboats).
   * Special gilded crops with mystical effects (**Luminous Straw**, **Glistering Melons**, **Auric Tubers**, **Gilded Beets**).
   * Throwable potion bottles, dual-axe combat mastery, and dynamic level-based discounts.

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide for Players

1. **Start Playing Naturally:**
   * Chop wood to level **Lumberjack**.
   * Mine ores and stones to level **Miner**.
   * Slay monsters with swords to level **Warrior**, or hunt with bows to level **Hunter**.
   * Harvest mature crops and breed livestock to level **Farmer**.
   * Read Dusty Books and craft enchantments to level **Enchanter**.

2. **Open the Sky Interface:**
   * Press `K` (default keybind, configurable under Controls -> Key Binds -> *Open Skills Tree*).
   * Use **A / D** or **Left / Right Arrow** keys (or scroll / drag) to navigate between constellations.
   * Hover over stars to inspect their requirements and perks.

3. **Enchanting Your First Item:**
   * Find a **Dusty Book** in dungeon, mineshaft, or stronghold chests.
   * Hold right-click to absorb the book's mystical glyphs into your mind.
   * Place an **Enchanting Table**, insert your tool or armor, select the discovered enchantment, provide the required crafting ingredients, and click **Enchant**!
